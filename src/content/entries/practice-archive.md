This website is itself an ongoing prototype.

The central question is how a personal practice can be represented without
dividing professional work, artwork, research and unfinished experiments into
separate identities.

## Current hypothesis

Entries should be connected through subjects, media, status and explicit
relationships. The archive should reveal continuity without flattening
different kinds of work into one generic project card.
