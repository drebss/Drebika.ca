I am a Libyan-Canadian artist and creative technologist based in Montreal.

My practice moves between image-making, spatial media, interfaces, artificial
intelligence and speculative systems. I am interested in the structures that
shape how people discover, interpret and relate to images and knowledge.

This site is not a finished portfolio. It is a working archive containing
commissioned projects, personal artwork, prototypes, research, concepts and
unfinished studies.
